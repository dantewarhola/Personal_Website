About

Background & skills

I'm a computer science major at the University of Pittsburgh focused on cybersecurity, risk management, and network analytics — and I'm seeking full-time roles in cybersecurity, network engineering, and penetration testing.

As a cybersecurity intern at DICK'S Sporting Goods, I assessed risk across external AI platforms and Shadow IT, built monitoring capabilities for the security team, and presented findings to cybersecurity leadership. Previously at TOPP Business Solutions I resolved 15–20 daily tickets for 100+ end users, deployed 30+ machines with standardized images, and cut repeat tickets 20% through documentation.

Outside of work I led risk management for Pi Kappa Phi across a year of weekly events with 250–400 attendees — coordinating with campus police, running incident-logging systems, and enforcing policy compliance. That mix of hands-on systems work and formal risk process is how I approach security: understand the attack surface, make what matters visible, and keep privilege tight.
